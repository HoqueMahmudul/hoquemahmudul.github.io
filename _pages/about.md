I am a Machine Learning Researcher at Morgan State University, focusing on interpretable, reliable, and resource-efficient generative and vision-language models for high-stakes scientific applications. I completed my M.S. in *Advanced Computing* at [Morgan State University](https://www.morgan.edu/) in 2025 under the supervision of [Dr. Md Mahmudur Rahman](https://www.morgan.edu/computer-science/faculty-and-staff/md-rahman).

My masters research was supported by a [National Science Foundation grant](https://www.nsf.gov/awardsearch/showAward?AWD_ID=2131207&HistoricalAwards=false) on deep learning-based medical image captioning, where I conducted an empirical investigation of vision–language models for automated radiology image captioning, optimizing architectures through parameter-efficient fine-tuning and quantization.

My current research investigates trustworthy explanations and evidence-based self-verification in medical vision-language models. I study how model-internal signals can connect generated statements to relevant image regions, whether those explanations faithfully reflect the model's computation, and how they might support error detection, calibrated confidence, and selective correction.

I also work on three-dimensional analysis of convective cloud cells using generative AI and deep learning. This project combines physics-based atmospheric simulation data, diffusion-generated synthetic cells, and 3D instance segmentation to distinguish individual cells within crowded convective systems. My work includes synthetic-data generation and validation, comparisons across segmentation architectures, and calibration of the instance-reconstruction pipeline to improve the reliability of cell-level analysis.

Previously, I worked as a research associate at the [National Institute of Standards and Technology](https://www.nist.gov/additive-manufacturing), developing a 3D-GAN framework to synthesize pore structures from X-ray computed tomography data. This work supported benchmark dataset development for additive-manufacturing defect analysis and nondestructive evaluation.

My other research includes knowledge-guided dermatology captioning, gastrointestinal visual question answering and synthetic image generation, and attribution of synthetic medical images to their source generative models. Across these projects, I am interested in understanding when models can be trusted, how their outputs can be evaluated, and how to make them useful under practical data and computing constraints.
